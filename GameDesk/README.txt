GameDesk - all your everyday and gaming widgets in one window
=============================================================

INSTALL
  1. Unzip this folder (right-click the zip > Extract All).
  2. Double-click "Install GameDesk.bat".
     If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" > "Run anyway".
  3. GameDesk opens. Next time, use the GameDesk icon on your Desktop or in the Start menu.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  Spotify controls (press play and Spotify starts hidden in the background, with album art), an AI chat (free local AI or Claude), CPU / RAM / disk, battery, ping, game launchers
  (Steam, Xbox, Ubisoft, Epic, ...), your installed Steam games, Windows quick tools,
  weather, to-do list, timer, notes and quick links. Click "Customize" to hide widgets.

NEEDS
  Windows 10 or 11 with Microsoft Edge (installed by default). Nothing else.

AI CHAT - FREE LOCAL AI
  The AI panel can use a free AI that runs on your own PC (private, works offline).
  1. Install Ollama (free) from https://ollama.com/download
  2. In GameDesk's AI panel, pick 'Local AI' and click 'Download (3.3 GB)'. Done.
  It runs on your processor; a PC with 16 GB of RAM or more is recommended.

AI CHAT - CLAUDE (OPTIONAL)
  For smarter answers, pick 'Claude' in the AI panel and paste a Claude API key.
  Get one at console.anthropic.com (Settings > API keys). API use is billed separately
  from a Claude Pro plan; a typical question costs about 1-3 cents. The key is stored
  encrypted on your PC only and is never included when you share GameDesk.

UNINSTALL
  Settings > Apps > Installed apps > GameDesk > Uninstall.

PRIVACY
  GameDesk only runs on your own PC (127.0.0.1). It doesn't send your data anywhere.
  Weather comes from open-meteo.com; Steam game pictures come from Steam.
  The local AI never sends anything off your PC. Claude chat messages go to
  Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) only when you send them.
